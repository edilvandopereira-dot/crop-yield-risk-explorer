Historical Brazilian soybean yield shortfalls in Mato Grosso, Rio Grande do Sul, Paraná and Goiás during 2016–2025. Each shortfall is a yield strictly more than 10 percent below the state's mean over the previous three years. Mato Grosso has 1 of 10 shortfall years and a worst gap of minus 11.8 percent, Rio Grande do Sul 3 of 10 and minus 46.2 percent, Paraná 2 of 10 and minus 30.5 percent, Goiás 0 of 10 and minus 6.7 percent. Historical frequencies are descriptive, not forecasts. Source: IBGE PAM, SIDRA 1612, snapshot retrieved on 16 October 2026.
