What this measure tells you

A transparent reference

For each state and year, the reference is the mean yield of the preceding three consecutive years. A shortfall occurs when observed yield is strictly more than the chosen percentage below that reference.

Gap = 100 × (observed yield / preceding three-year mean − 1). The current year never enters its own baseline.

A useful first question

Use the explorer to identify regional histories worth investigating before a sourcing or agronomic study. Compare scale, frequency, severity and sample size together. The table does not recommend suppliers or allocate investments.

Limits that affect the decision

State averages hide variation among municipalities and farms. Production exposure is not financial loss. Shortfall frequency is not an estimated probability for the next harvest. Weather, management, crop mix and measurement revisions can all affect the series; this dataset cannot isolate their causal contributions.

The baseline adjusts gradually and can lag a trend. This is a retrospective evaluation using today's revised data, not a forecast backtest using each year's original release. No claim of model skill is made.

Source, quality checks and data conventions

Source: IBGE Municipal Agricultural Production (PAM), SIDRA table 1612, category 2713 (soybeans, grain), annual data, Brazil and 27 states, 2010–2025. Yield is reported in kg/ha and displayed in t/ha. Production is in tonnes; areas are in hectares.

The pipeline checks source hashes, unique state-year-variable keys, the complete grid, state sums against the national totals, and yield against production/harvested area with a 2 kg/ha rounding tolerance. The source values are retained alongside the transformed values.

SIDRA “-” denotes absolute zero. “...” means unavailable, “..” not applicable and “X” withheld. A yield with no harvested area is undefined and is excluded, not imputed. A rolling baseline requires three observed consecutive years.
